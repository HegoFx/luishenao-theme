I'm Luis Henao. I work with one kind of business only: independent custom closet companies in the United States. My job is to get homeowners in your area to text you after they've already confirmed budget, timeline and location, so you spend your time on what actually makes money: designing, selling and installing.

## Why I only work with closet companies

A custom closet isn't an impulse buy. Tickets run $5,000 to $8,000 or more, the decision takes weeks and there's almost always an in-home visit before anyone signs. A generic ad pulls in people who just want to "get a price," and that costs you hours of driving and estimates that never close.

Because I focus on one niche, I know which photos make a homeowner stop scrolling, which questions they ask before they request an estimate and which signals separate a buyer from someone browsing. I don't have to learn your business from scratch on your dime.

## What working with me looks like

- You talk to me, not to an account manager who changes every three months.
- I work with a small number of companies on purpose, so I can review your leads every day.
- I explain the numbers in plain English: how many homeowners came in, what each one cost and how many booked.
- If something isn't working, you hear it from me before you notice it yourself.
- The ad account is yours. If we ever stop working together, everything stays with you.

## Based in Colombia, on your hours

I work from Colombia on US business hours, in English and in Spanish. You get a specialist who answers during your day at a fraction of what a US agency charges. And in markets like Florida and Texas, where a big share of homeowners are Hispanic, I can run ads and follow-up in Spanish too, which reaches buyers your competitors are ignoring.

## What I don't do

- I don't promise a number of sales. I commit to delivering pre-qualified homeowners; closing the job is still your craft, and you're better at it than anyone.
- I don't lock you into a long contract. The pilot is one month.
- I don't take a commission on your sales.
- I don't post on your social accounts. Keep doing it while the ads run, because the two together work much better.
