We don't guarantee sales or revenue. These are the full terms of the 3 guarantees shown on the page.

## 1. Live in 21 days, or your first month back

- "Live" means ads that are active and approved by Meta.
- The 21 days start when you've sent us your account access and business info.
- If we miss it, we refund your first month and you're free from the 3-month minimum.
- If Meta rejects something because of the type of business or your account history, the clock pauses while it gets resolved [CONFIRM].
- You approve creatives within 3 business days [CONFIRM timeframe]. Waiting days don't count.

## 2. 20 pre-qualified homeowners in 60 days, or month 3 is free

A pre-qualified homeowner is a person who:

- Owns the home.
- Is inside your service area.
- Left a valid phone number and email.
- Answered in the form their budget range, the type of project (walk-in, reach-in, garage, pantry or other) and when they want to do it.

If you set a minimum budget at kickoff, only those who meet it count [CONFIRM default floor, if any].

These don't count: duplicates, fake or disconnected numbers, people outside your area, renters, and homeowners you didn't contact within 48 hours.

- The 60 days start when your ads go live, not when you sign.
- Requires at least $1,200/month in active ad spend with no pauses, and that you don't edit or turn off ads on your own.
- If we miss it, your third month is free, applied as a credit on that invoice.
- It doesn't include booked estimates, sales or revenue. We deliver the homeowner; booking the estimate is on you.

## 3. Your ad account, your ads and your CRM are yours

- Your ad account and every ad we made are yours. If you leave, you take them with you.
- The CRM runs on our account during your first year and is included. If you leave earlier, you keep it until that year ends.
- You can export your contacts anytime.
- After the first year, if you want to keep the CRM, we coordinate and quote it separately [CONFIRM amount].

## Commitment

3-month minimum. After that, it's month to month. Cancel with 30 days' notice.
