**Short answer:** Angi Leads can work for a custom closet company that needs volume right away and answers every lead within minutes. For most independent closet businesses, though, the shared-lead model means you pay to compete on price against two or three other pros for the same homeowner, and that makes each signed job far more expensive than the per-lead price suggests.

This review looks at Angi from the point of view of a closet company owner: how it works, what it really costs per job, where it helps and where it hurts.

## How Angi Leads works for contractors

Angi Leads is the pro side of Angi, the company formerly known as HomeAdvisor and Angie's List. A homeowner fills out a request, for example "custom closet, walk-in, Tampa," and Angi sends that request to pros who cover that category and zip code. You pay for each lead you receive, whether or not the homeowner ever answers your call.

Two details matter more than anything else:

- **Leads are usually shared.** The same request typically goes to several pros at once. The homeowner hears from three or four companies in the first hour.
- **Price depends on category and area.** Contractors commonly report paying anywhere from about $15 to well over $100 per lead, plus membership or subscription fees. Always check the current pricing inside your own pro account before you budget.

## The real cost: per job, not per lead

The per-lead price is the number Angi shows you. The number that matters is what you pay in leads for every closet you actually sell. Here's an example with round numbers, not a promise of what you'll see:

| | Example |
|---|---|
| Price per lead | $60 |
| Leads that answer and book an estimate | 1 in 4 |
| Estimates that turn into a signed job | 1 in 4 |
| Leads needed per signed job | 16 |
| Lead cost per signed job | $960 |

On a $6,000 closet that's 16% of the ticket gone before materials, labor or your own time driving to estimates. And because the homeowner is collecting several quotes, the jobs you do win often come with pressure to lower your price.

## Where Angi Leads helps a closet company

- **Speed to market.** You can start getting requests within days, with no ads to build.
- **No marketing skills needed.** You don't have to learn targeting, creative or tracking.
- **Trust.** Some homeowners only feel comfortable hiring through a brand they know.
- **Filling gaps.** In a slow month, a few extra estimates can keep your installers busy.

## Where it hurts

- **You race against other pros.** The first company to call usually gets the appointment, so you have to drop everything to answer.
- **Price shoppers.** People who request through a marketplace are often comparing, not deciding.
- **You pay for silence.** Leads who never pick up still cost you. Credit or refund policies exist but are limited and take your time to request.
- **You build someone else's brand.** Every job you win through Angi makes Angi stronger, not your own name.
- **No control.** You can't choose the photos, the message or the questions the homeowner sees before they reach you.

## A better alternative for independent closet companies

The main fix is to own your lead source. When you run your own Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram) to homeowners in your service area, three things change:

1. **The lead is only yours.** Nobody else gets the same homeowner at the same time.
2. **You filter before you talk.** Ask budget, timeline and zip code up front, so tire kickers drop off on their own.
3. **They see your work first.** Your ads show your own installs, so the homeowner already likes what you build before the first call.

Running ads takes work: creative, a good form, fast follow-up and weekly optimization. That's exactly the part I take off your plate in the pilot, so you only talk to homeowners who are ready.

## Verdict

Angi Leads is worth it if you're brand new, need volume this month and have someone who can call every lead within five minutes. If you're an established independent company selling 3 to 8 closets a month, you'll usually get more profit per job from exclusive, pre-qualified leads you own.

## Frequently asked questions

?? How much do Angi leads cost for closet companies?
It depends on your category and zip code. Contractors commonly report from about $15 to over $100 per lead, plus membership fees. Check the current price in your Angi Pro account, and judge it by cost per signed job, not cost per lead.

?? Are Angi leads exclusive?
Usually not. Most Angi leads are shared with several pros who cover the same service and area, which is why response speed and price pressure matter so much.

?? Can I get a refund for bad Angi leads?
Angi has a credit request process for some lead problems, such as wrong contact information. It's limited, it isn't automatic and it takes your time, so don't count on it as part of your budget.

?? What's the best alternative to Angi for a custom closet company?
Exclusive leads from your own Meta ads, pre-qualified by budget, timeline and location before they reach you. You keep the lead, the brand and the ad account.
